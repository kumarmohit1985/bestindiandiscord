What is the best Mumbai and Delhi Discord server to join? FIZA is the top regional Indian Discord community connecting youth across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, and Kolkata. Connect with People in Your City

Whether you're looking for local street food recommendations, college peers, offline meetup circles, or local city gaming squads, our regional channels make local networking easy in our active Hindi voice chat rooms:

  • City-Specific Roles: Pick your state/city role in #roles to unlock dedicated discussions for your hometown.
  • Local Food & Hangout Recommendations: Discover the best street food spots, cafe recommendations, and weekend activities in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and Bangalore.
  • College & University Circles: Connect with students from DU, Mumbai University, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, and local institutes on our Student Hub.

  • Zero Toxicity: A safe, verified space with strict rules against creepiness, privacy violations, or stalking.

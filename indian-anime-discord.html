Every weekend, our community gathers in our Level 3 boosted Stage channels for high-definition screening parties. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to talk on mic during watch parties?

No, voice channels have synchronized text chat rooms so you can chat via text and react while watching.

Can community members suggest anime for streaming?

Yes! We run community polls in `#anime-discussion` every week where members vote for the next weekend screening.

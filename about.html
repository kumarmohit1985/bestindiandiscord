About TheIndia

Founded with a vision to connect Desis across the globe, TheIndia is more than a server—it’s a home away from home built on respect, gaming passion, and cultural camaraderie.

🌐 Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam

Inspired by the timeless belief that the world is one family, our primary goal is building a respectful, inclusive atmosphere where everyone feels welcomed from day one.

🛡️ 24/7 Toxic-Free Moderation

We leverage a blended system of automated bot security and active human moderators to guarantee zero-tolerance for toxicity, harassment, and spam.

🎙️ Community-First Culture

Whether you're grinding Valorant, participating in Bollywood movie marathons, or sharing late-night chai thoughts, there is a dedicated space for your voice.

🚀 Fast-Growing Ecosystem

Home to 18,000+ passionate members, we host weekly voice events, gaming tournaments, and Nitro giveaways to reward active participants.

⚖️ Ban & Kick Appeals

If your account was banned or kicked from TheIndia and you believe the action was a misunderstanding or you would like to appeal for a second chance, please review the requirements below.

How to Submit an Appeal:

Send an email to officialtheindia@gmail.com with the subject line "Ban Appeal - [Your Discord Username]" including the following details:

  • Discord Username & User ID (e.g., username#0000 or 18-digit ID).
  • Approximate Date & Reason for the ban or kick.
  • Why you believe the punishment should be reconsidered or what steps you have taken to ensure it won't happen again.

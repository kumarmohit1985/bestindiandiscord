Study Smart with a Focused Indian Community

Self-studying for competitive examinations or college degrees can be isolating. Our academic channels give you the structure and mutual peer accountability needed to maintain high productivity without intrusive distractions:

  • Silent & Lo-Fi Voice Lounges: Mute-only and camera-enabled study rooms with background study beats from our Lo-Fi Music Lounges.
  • Automated Pomodoro Bots: Timed 25/5 and 50/10 focus intervals to build consistent study discipline.
  • Regional Study Circles: Form local study groups with college peers from Delhi NCR (DU, IPU, IIT Delhi) and Mumbai through our Delhi NCR & Mumbai Hub.
  • Late-Night Study Motivation: Active voice channels where students pull productive night sessions together in our 24/7 late night voice lounges.
  • Post-Study Gaming & Chill: Once study targets are hit, unwind in our Roblox & Minecraft SMP lobbies.
  • Zero Distractions: Separate isolated channels away from the main gaming and chat sections.

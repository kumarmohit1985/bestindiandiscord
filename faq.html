Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating and participating in India's top Discord community.

🔗 How do I join TheIndia Discord server?

Click on any Join Now button on this site or use the direct invite discord.gg/theindia. Once inside, complete the quick onboarding rules check to get full access.

🌸 Is the community safe for female members?

Yes. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against toxicity, harassment, and creepy DMs. Our Desi moderation team and automod systems operate 24/7 to maintain a welcoming atmosphere.

🎮 Which games are actively played?

We have dedicated looking-for-group (LFG) channels and custom roles for Valorant, BGMI, GTA V RP, Minecraft SMP, and party games.

🎙️ Are voice channels active round the clock?

Yes! Members are in Hindi and English voice channels 24/7—from afternoon study sessions to 3 AM late-night chilling rooms.

💎 What are the booster and VIP perks?

Server boosters unlock exclusive custom profile badges, private voice room controls, VIP chatrooms, and 2x winning odds in our weekly Discord Nitro giveaways.
